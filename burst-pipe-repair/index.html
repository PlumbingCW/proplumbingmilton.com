Emergency steps

Pipe burst and water is flowing: shut it off now

A burst pipe can release many gallons a minute at normal house pressure, enough to bring down a ceiling in less time than a phone call. This is the order that protects the house while the plumber we dispatch is on the way, any hour of the day or night.

  1. Walk to the main shutoff valve and close it by turning the wheel clockwise to the stop. A lever valve closes with a quarter turn.
  2. Open the lowest faucet in the house, then one upstairs, to drain the pipes and ease pressure at the break.
  3. If the failure is in a hot water line, switch off the water heater at its power switch or breaker, or turn the gas control to pilot, so the tank does not run dry and overheat.
  4. Stay out of any room where water is reaching outlets, the panel or light fixtures, and call the electric utility if the panel is wet.
  5. Mop or bail standing water that you can reach safely, and open windows to start drying the air.

What counts as a burst, cracked or broken pipe

A true burst is a split along the pipe wall, often a lengthwise tear a few inches long. A crack is thinner and may seep for days before it opens. A broken pipe is one that has separated at a fitting or been struck, as when a nail goes through a wall or a shovel hits a buried line.

Pipe leak repair covers the small end of that range: pinholes, weeping joints, a pinched copper elbow. Leak repair often turns into a search first, so if you cannot find the wet spot, our leak detection page explains how hidden water gets located before anything is cut open.

Copper pipe repair: pinholes, splits and bad joints

Copper is the most common supply pipe in houses that have been updated. It lasts a long time, but water chemistry, velocity and rough soldering can pit it from the inside until a pinhole opens. A hole the size of a pencil lead can soak a wall cavity in a day.

A repair cuts out the damaged piece, cleans both ends and joins new pipe with solder or a push-fit coupling. Several pinholes in one stretch usually mean the whole run is failing, and patching each hole costs more time than replacing the run.

Drain pipe repair: when the cracked pipe carries waste

A cracked drain pipe leaks wastewater, not clean water. Signs are a sewer smell near a wall, a soft floor spot, stains under a bathroom or a drain that backs up as well as leaks. Rust, root pressure and settling crack cast iron, clay and old plastic.

A camera shows the crack and its position, and the plumber replaces only the damaged section when the rest is sound. Drain lines that fail along the length may call for sewer line repair instead of a patch. Do not use water fixtures that feed a cracked drain until it is fixed.

Frozen pipes: only in freezing weather

Water expands as it turns to ice, and the ice plug builds pressure that splits the pipe nearby, not necessarily at the frozen spot. Pipes on exterior walls, in unheated crawlspaces and in garages freeze first. The warning sign is a faucet that runs weakly or not at all while others work.

If you suspect a freeze, leave the faucet open, shut the main valve and warm the area gently with a hair dryer. Never use an open flame. If a pipe already split, water will pour out as the ice melts, so keep the main closed and call.

After the water stops: cleanup and insurance steps

The first day decides how much damage turns permanent. Work through these once the main is closed.

  1. Photograph and video everything

    Record the broken pipe, the wet areas and damaged belongings before moving anything.

  2. Keep electricity out of wet areas

    Do not touch wet outlets or appliances standing in water. Call an electrician if wiring was soaked.

  3. Start drying

    Run fans and dehumidifiers and open windows. Wet drywall and insulation that stay damp for days invite mold.

  4. Call your insurer

    Report the claim early and ask what they need, since policy wording decides what is covered.

Permits: what replacing a pipe requires in Massachusetts

Only a master or journeyman plumber licensed by the state may do plumbing work. The local plumbing inspector must issue a permit before piping is replaced, altered or relocated, and permits go only to licensed plumbers. Minor repairs such as a leaky faucet do not need one. You can ask to see the permit, and the Mass.gov licence lookup lets you check a plumber's license and discipline history.

For fee and inspection details, ask the Milton plumbing inspector. That permit rule follows the Massachusetts Uniform State Plumbing Code, 248 CMR 10.00, which is the standard the local plumbing inspector applies.

What affects the price of burst pipe repair

A clear price comes first, before any pipe is cut. These drivers set it.

Pipe material and size

Copper, PEX and cast iron take different parts and different labor, and larger diameters cost more to fit.

Access to the break

A pipe in an open basement ceiling is quick. A pipe inside a finished wall needs an opening cut and patched.

Length of damaged pipe

One split takes a coupling. A corroded run may need several feet or an entire branch replaced.

Emergency timing

Nights, weekends and holidays are priced differently from a scheduled visit, and the plumber explains this up front.

On insurance: a sudden burst is the kind of loss many policies address, while gradual leaks and wear usually fall outside. Confirm with your insurer before repairs are billed.
