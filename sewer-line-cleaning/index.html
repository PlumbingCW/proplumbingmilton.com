Who maintains which part of the sewer

A house sewer has two parts. The private pipe runs from the building to the property line or the street connection. The public main runs under the street. Where one ends and the other begins differs from address to address, so do not guess.

Ask Milton Water and Sewer which section is whose before you pay for work. If the stoppage is in the public main, the Town handles it. If it is on your side, a licensed plumber clears it.

Storm surge and sewer backups in East Milton

The Town of Milton Community Resilience Building Workshop summary from April 2020 described storm surge in East Milton backing sewers up into homes. It also named the Neponset River banks, Turner's Pond, Pine Tree Brook and Unquity Brook as areas with flooding.

If your backup arrives with a storm or a very high tide, cleaning your own line may not stop it, because the public sewer itself can be overloaded. In that case, call a plumber to confirm your side is clear, call Milton Water and Sewer to report it, and ask a plumber about a backwater valve on the house drain. How the plumber clears a main sewer line

  1. Locate the access

    The plumber finds the cleanout, or removes a toilet if there is none, to get a cable or hose into the main.

  2. Run a drum machine

    A heavy cable with a cutting head breaks up roots, paper and grease along the way.

  3. Follow with water

    A jet or a hose flush carries cuttings out so they do not form a new plug a few feet away. Hydro jetting is used when buildup lines the wall.

  4. Flow test

    Water runs through the house drains to confirm everything empties at full speed.

  5. Offer a camera look

    If the line was badly blocked or has clogged before, the camera shows why.
Emergency steps

When sewage is coming up in the house

Wastewater inside the house is a health hazard and a same-night problem. Call at any hour and we dispatch through the night and on weekends. While waiting, these actions are safe:

  1. Stop all water use. That means no flushing, no laundry and no dishwasher.
  2. Keep kids and pets away. Raw sewage carries bacteria, so wash hands well after any contact.
  3. Wear rubber boots and gloves if you must enter, and never touch electrical items in wet areas.
  4. Open windows for ventilation in the affected room.
  5. Photograph the damage for your insurer before you clean up, then move belongings to dry spots.

What affects the price of sewer line cleaning

Cleaning a main line can range from a short job to a long one with root cutting and a follow-up flush. You get a clear price before work starts. The drivers are:

Access

An outside cleanout makes the work faster than removing a toilet.

Blockage type

Paper and grease clear faster than thick roots, which need cutting heads and extra passes.

Line length and pipe size

Longer runs to the street need more cable and more time.

Camera look

A recording adds time but reveals damage that changes the plan.

Standard homeowners policies often cover sudden and accidental water damage but usually not wear, gradual leaks or maintenance, and many exclude sewer backup unless an endorsement was added. Policy wording decides, so read it or ask the insurer.

Keeping the sewer line clear afterward

Do not flush wipes, even those labeled flushable. Keep grease out of kitchen drains, and scrape plates before washing. If you have large trees near the line, ask a plumber to camera the pipe every so often. We also serve Randolph and other towns around Milton with the same emergency line.
