Call the 24/7 line

The fastest way to reach a plumber is the phone: (617) 752-0573. The line is answered nights, weekends and holidays. There is no contact form on this site, because a form cannot tell you how to shut off a main valve while water is running.

Email is for business questions only, at contact@proplumbingmilton.com. A plumbing problem should always start with a call.

Where we are

Milton Pro Plumbing, 2 Granite Ave, Milton, MA 02186. The address is the dispatch base, and the map below shows it. Plumbers travel to you, so there is no walk-in counter.

What to have ready when you call

A few details speed up the call and help the plumber arrive with the right parts.

  • Your address and the nearest cross street.
  • What you can see, hear or smell, and when it started.
  • Whether water is still running and whether you have found the main shutoff.
  • The age and fuel type of a water heater or boiler, if that is the problem.
  • Whether you own the property or are calling for a tenant or landlord.

What happens after you call

The dispatcher confirms your address, asks what you see and sends a licensed plumber. You are told what to expect for your address and you hear the price before any work starts.

If the job turns out to need more than first thought, such as a permit or a second visit for parts, the plumber explains why and you decide before the extra work begins.

Safety first while you wait

If water is running, close the main shutoff valve, usually near the meter where the line enters the basement. Open one faucet on the lowest floor to drain the pipes. Stay out of rooms where water has reached outlets, and move valuables up off the floor.

If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 or your gas utility from outside. Do not turn lights or appliances on or off on your way out.

Property managers and landlords

You can call for a tenant's emergency. Give the unit, the problem and who can give access. The plumber confirms the price with you before work starts, and the dispatcher can note a different billing contact.

Towns covered

The line serves Milton and dispatches to Canton, Quincy, Randolph and Braintree. If your address is in a Boston neighborhood such as Dorchester, Mattapan or Hyde Park, call and the dispatcher tells you whether it is covered.
