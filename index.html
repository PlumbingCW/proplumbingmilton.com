Local notes

Plumbing in Milton: what the town's pipes and rivers mean for you

Milton's housing is old. The Town's 2020 Housing Production Plan found that 46.8 percent of homes were built in 1939 or earlier and another 28.5 percent between 1940 and 1959, with a median home age of 71 years. For you that means cast iron drains, galvanized supply pipes and original shutoff valves are common, so a visit often turns into a conversation about what to replace now and what can wait.

Water comes only from the MWRA, distributed by the Milton DPW. The MWRA notes that old water pipes can develop tuberculation, which can cause an occasional red, yellow or brown color in the water. If that is happening at one tap, the fix is usually inside the house. If it shows at every tap, call Milton Water and Sewer at 617-696-5731.

Flooding shapes the calls in some parts of town. The Town's 2020 resilience report names the banks of the Neponset River, the areas near Turner's Pond, Pine Tree Brook and Unquity Brook, and roads such as Central Avenue, Granite Avenue and Brook Road as flood-prone. It also describes storm surge in East Milton backing sewers up into homes. A sump pump with a battery backup is the practical answer for a wet basement in those pockets.

The neighborhoods run from Milton Village on the Neponset at the Boston line to East Milton Square near Quincy, with Milton Hill, Brush Hill and western Milton along Canton Avenue in between. Dorchester and Mattapan lie across the river to the north and Hyde Park lies to the west. Those are Boston neighborhoods, so call and the dispatcher tells you whether your address is covered.

Pricing

What drives the price of a plumbing job

No two plumbing problems cost the same, so this site does not print price ranges. What it does is tell you what moves the number, so the quote you hear makes sense. The plumber gives you the actual figure before any work starts.

A clog at a sink trap is quick. A clog in the main line under the basement slab takes more time and equipment, and the price follows.

Corroded valves and brittle fittings in older homes can turn a small repair into a larger one. The plumber tells you before opening the wall.

A water heater, a pump or a section of pipe is priced by size and type. A different fuel or venting setup changes the parts list.

Work behind finished walls or under slabs takes longer. Where state rules require a permit, the licensed plumber pulls it.

Many homeowners policies cover sudden and accidental water damage but not wear, gradual leaks or maintenance. Policy wording decides, so read yours or ask the insurer.
